About Us

Campus Marketplace is a student-exclusive marketplace designed to make buying and selling simple, secure, and affordable. Students often have items they no longer use—such as textbooks, calculators, bicycles, electronics, or hostel essentials—while others need these items at reasonable prices. Our platform connects buyers and sellers within the campus community, creating a trusted environment where students can communicate directly and complete transactions conveniently. We believe in sustainability, affordability, and helping students make the most of their resources.
